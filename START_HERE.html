From Zero to AI
12 Projects · 1 Journey

Every project here was built for this seminar. By the end of today you'll see how a simple web page grows into AI agents that think and act on their own.

❓ What is a project?

A project is a real, working solution to a real problem — built with code. Not theory, not notes: something people can use, see and click.

🔥 Why does it matter?

  • Recruiters ask "show me what you built", not "what did you study"
  • It proves skill better than any marksheet
  • Your GitHub = your new resume
  • Learning by building sticks 10× better

🧭 How to choose?

Love design? → Web. Love logic & numbers? → Python & Data. Want the future? → AI, Agents, Agentic AI. Every road starts with fundamentals.
